ABOUT BCA

WHO WE ARE

Led by 8 E-board members, BCA brings Babson's consulting community together through CORE (Consulting Orientation & Readiness Experience) and the Undergraduate Consulting Program (UCP), along with events, workshops, and opportunities to connect with professionals and alumni.

OUR VISION
