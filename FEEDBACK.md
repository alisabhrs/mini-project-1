## 2. Class Showcase Feedback

During the class showcase, I received a lot of positive feedback about the overall design, organization, and interactive features of my website.

### What People Liked

- The green color palette fits Babson's branding.
- The homepage looks professional and visually appealing.
- The flipping cards, carousel, and stacking sections make the website more interactive.
- The programs and resources are clearly organized.
- The website communicates BCA's purpose and identity well.
- The navigation is easy to follow.

### Suggestions for Improvement

- Add more photos of BCA members, executive board members, and events.
- Improve typography and text sizing to make the website feel more natural.
- Simplify some sections of the homepage to reduce scrolling.
- Improve text readability and contrast.
- Fix the statistics counters displaying zero.
- Replace placeholder images with actual BCA photos.
- Add application dates and links to the Programs page.
- Organize CSS, JavaScript, and images into separate folders.

Overall, the feedback helped me understand how I could make the website more authentic, functional, and user-friendly.
