# Mini Project 1 Proposal

## Client Brief

Client: Ninda, President of the Babson Consulting Association (BCA)

### Purpose

The website will serve as the central online home for the Babson Consulting Association. It will introduce students to BCA, explain its programs and events, showcase what the organization does, and make consulting feel more accessible to Babson students.

More detailed resources, such as casebooks, recruitment trackers, consulting frameworks, and interview-preparation materials, will be stored on BCA's Babson SharePoint. The website will provide a clear link directing Babson students to this Resource Hub.

### Audience

The primary audience is Babson undergraduate students, including students who are completely new to consulting as well as students who are actively preparing for consulting recruitment.

The website should be welcoming and approachable to students regardless of their previous consulting experience.

### Key Action

The main action is for visitors to explore BCA's opportunities and find ways to get involved.

The primary call-to-action will be:

**"Explore BCA"**

Other important actions include:

- View Our Programs
- Explore Upcoming Events
- Access the Resource Hub
- Join Our Mailing List
- Follow BCA on Instagram and LinkedIn
- Partner With BCA

The Resource Hub will direct Babson students to BCA's SharePoint, where they may need to sign in using their Babson account.

## Pages Needed

For the initial version of the website, the five priority pages will be:

### Home

A clear introduction to BCA, its mission, upcoming opportunities, and links to the most important areas of the website.

### About

Information about BCA's mission, vision, values, and commitment to making consulting knowledge more accessible and inclusive for Babson students.

### Programs

Information about BCA's programs, including the Undergraduate Consulting Program and any future programs. Each program can include a description, timeline, eligibility information, and application link when available.

### Resources

An introduction to the resources available to Babson students, including casebooks, consulting frameworks, interview-preparation materials, and the consulting recruitment tracker.

This page will direct students to BCA's SharePoint Resource Hub. It may also include a "Coming Soon" preview of BCA's original Framework Book.

### Contact

BCA's email, Instagram, LinkedIn, interest form, and information for businesses, alumni, and consulting firms interested in collaborating with BCA.

## Future Sections

If the website expands beyond the initial project scope, additional sections could include:

- Events
- Our Team
- Alumni and Partners
- BCA Framework Book

The website should be designed so that future BCA E-board members can continue adding programs, events, resources, and accomplishments.

## Content Status

BCA currently has or can provide:

- BCA logo
- Brand colors and existing social media designs
- Mission and vision language
- Program descriptions
- E-board names, roles, and headshots
- Event posters and photographs
- Instagram and LinkedIn links
- BCA contact information
- SharePoint Resource Hub link
- Program application links and forms
- Information about the upcoming BCA Framework Book

Some website copy and updated photographs may still need to be developed.

## Style Preferences

The website should feel:

- Modern
- Professional
- Welcoming
- Credible
- Clean and easy to navigate
- Student-centered
- Energetic without feeling childish

The goal is to create a website with the polish and professionalism associated with consulting while still feeling approachable and connected to the Babson student community.

The website should use BCA's existing brand colors consistently, have clear typography, and use enough white space to keep the design organized and easy to navigate.

The design should avoid:

- Cluttered layouts
- Large amounts of text on one page
- Generic corporate stock photos
- Excessive animations
- Too many colors
- Inconsistent fonts
- Dark or intimidating designs
- An overly serious or corporate appearance
- Stereotypical consulting imagery such as handshakes, skyscrapers, or people pointing at random charts

## Inspiration and Creative Direction

Ninda did not have a specific website or layout that she wanted BCA's website to copy. She referenced professional consulting organizations and firms, including Boston Consulting Group, as general inspiration for the polished and professional feeling of the website.

However, she gave me creative freedom over the specific layout and visual direction. Rather than recreating another consulting website, the goal is to create a design that feels unique to BCA while maintaining the organization, clarity, and professionalism associated with consulting.

The design will follow the preferences discussed during the interview: modern, professional, welcoming, clean, student-centered, and easy to navigate. It should feel polished without becoming overly corporate or intimidating.

I will use these guidelines, BCA's existing branding, and my own design research to develop the final layout and visual direction.

## Additional Requirements

The website should also include:

- A mobile-friendly design
- Clear navigation and buttons
- A direct link or QR code to the SharePoint Resource Hub
- An area for upcoming events and deadlines
- Instagram and LinkedIn links
- A mailing-list or interest-form sign-up
- Information for businesses, alumni, and firms interested in partnering with BCA
- A "Coming Soon" preview of the BCA Framework Book
- A disclaimer identifying BCA as a student-led organization at Babson College, if appropriate

The website should be designed for long-term use so future BCA boards can continue updating its content.

## Homepage Direction

The homepage should immediately communicate what BCA does and make consulting feel approachable to Babson students.

The proposed main statement is:

> **Your consulting journey starts here.**

Supporting text:

> **Explore consulting, build practical skills, and connect with opportunities through the Babson Consulting Association.**

A prominent **"Explore BCA"** button will direct visitors toward BCA's programs and opportunities.

The homepage will also provide clear paths to programs, resources, upcoming opportunities, and ways to get involved.

## Layout Plan

The website will use consistent navigation and styling across all pages.

The homepage will likely include:

1. Header and navigation
2. Hero section with BCA's main message and "Explore BCA" button
3. Short introduction to BCA
4. Programs and opportunities preview
5. Upcoming events or deadlines
6. Resource Hub callout
7. BCA Framework Book "Coming Soon" preview
8. Ways to get involved
9. Footer with contact information and social media links

The other pages will follow the same visual system so the website feels consistent throughout.

The layout will prioritize shorter, clearly separated sections instead of large blocks of text. It will also be responsive so that the website is easy to use on both desktop and mobile devices.
